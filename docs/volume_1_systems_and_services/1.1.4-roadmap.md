## 1.1.4.1 How to read it

This is the one ordered list of fabric's work (owner request 2026-10-04, after the alignment to the global rules,
5.1.1). The phases (1.1.3), the federation milestones (1.8.7), the Samba build plan (2.11.1.12) and the deferred
work (5.4.1) say *what* each piece is; **this chapter alone says in which order they are built**. A change of
order is an owner decision, recorded here with its date. Each item keeps the name it has in its own chapter.

## 1.1.4.5 Names that look alike

- **F1–F4 phases** (1.8.7.1: trust and names, identity, HA, relays) are not **decisions F1–F10** (1.2.4, D60–D69).
  "F3" in 5.4.1 and 2.11.1 means **decision F3**, sharing between sites; the HA work is **phase F3**.
- **S1–S6 decisions** (1.2.6) are not the **S0–S8 build steps** (2.11.1.12).
- **M-milestones** (1.8.7) number the federation build; **phases** (1.1.3) number the product's first build.
