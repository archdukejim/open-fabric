## 4.6.1.1 `--keycloak-sync`
Re-run `keycloak_bootstrap.py`: realm, LDAP federation to the domain (Samba AD, [1.6.2](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.6.2-keycloak.md)), group mapper and sync, `fabric-admin` role → `admins` group, the `fabric-webui` OIDC client and its TOTP flow. Idempotent — use after changing `webui_*` vars or to repair drift made in the admin console. It reads fabric's secrets, so OpenBao must be unlocked.

```bash
sudo fabricctl --keycloak-sync
```
