## 1.1.7.1 Policy

The home chapter for third-party licences (5.8), from decision 2.1.1.25 (owner, 2026-10-03):

- fabric is MIT-licensed (`LICENSE`).
- Code fabric ships in its own package or copies into its pages — today two JavaScript libraries — must be under
  a permissive licence (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC).
- Programs fabric runs in containers (BIND, Kea, Keycloak, Samba, …) keep their own licences, the GPL
  included: they run as separate, unmodified programs from their distributions or upstream projects.

## 1.1.7.2 Attribution

Every bundled library is listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` at the repository root, with its version, source, copyright
and licence text, and in `static/vendor/README.md` with the SHA-256 of the copy. Adding or upgrading one means
updating both in the same change. The package ships `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` with the licence.
