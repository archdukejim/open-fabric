# 2.1.5 Decisions: Certificates: Step-CA

Topics:
- 2.1.5.1 Name-constrain each site's intermediate to `<site>.<domain>`?
- 2.1.5.2 Revocation of a removed site's intermediate
- 2.1.5.3 Let's Encrypt through Cloudflare

## 2.1.5.1 Name-constrain each site's intermediate to `<site>.<domain>`?

- **Decision:** Proposed: yes for DNS names; people and device certificates use non-DNS names, so check the constraint set against them before deciding.
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.9
- **Made:** proposed 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-02, with the federation design; never marked decided.
- **Why:** a site's CA should not be able to issue for another site's names (1.9).
- **Status:** Open: the owner has not answered it.

## 2.1.5.2 Revocation of a removed site's intermediate

- **Decision:** Proposed: a CRL from the root site's CA, published on `certs.<domain>` and checked by every site's FreeRADIUS and nginx (today there is no CRL: fabric relies on unlinking devices).
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.9
- **Made:** proposed 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-02, with the federation design; never marked decided.
- **Why:** a removed site's intermediate must stop being trusted everywhere (1.9).
- **Status:** Open: the owner has not answered it.

## 2.1.5.3 Let's Encrypt through Cloudflare

- **Decision:** The DC's certificates come from Step-CA like every service; external CAs and Let's Encrypt are a separate milestone.
- **Shapes:** 1.5, 1.6
- **Made:** 2026-10-04, by the owner, answering the Samba design's questions (proposed: its own design after this one, unless pulled forward).
- **Why:** 2.3.6.1.1. Let's Encrypt through Cloudflare's DNS-01 is its own milestone, after 1.0 (1.1.4).
- **Status:** In force.
