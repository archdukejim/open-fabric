## 2.1.15.1 Central logging

- **Decision:** Fluent Bit as an optional stack component (`install_fluentbit`, chosen at setup, hot-addable): forwards all logs to syslog (RFC 5424, TLS) and/or Elasticsearch/OpenSearch, disk-buffered, credentials in OpenBao ([1.15.1.1](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.15.1-fluent-bit.md#11511-log-forwarding-decided-21151-built)).
- **Shapes:** 1.15
- **Made:** 2026-09-27 to 2026-10-01, while fabric was designed, by the owner.
- **Why:** logs leave the host for whatever central store a network already has.
- **Status:** In force; built.
