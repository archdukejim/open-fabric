## 2.1.12.1 Is the DNS filter part of an install, and what does it start with

- **Decision:** **On by default** (`dns_filter: adguard`; `none` turns it off), shown on setup's plan and asked in Advanced. A first deploy sends internet lookups to **Cloudflare** over DNS-over-HTTPS (by address) and turns on the **AdGuard DNS filter** list ([1.12.1.3](../volume_1_description_and_architecture/1.12.1-adguard.md#11213-settings)).
- **Shapes:** 1.12, 3.3
- **Made:** 2026-10-07, by the owner, in 0.6.0. Replaces the 2026-10-01 choice of local DNS only.
- **Why:** the owner expected the filter in the stack; [2.3.6.1.27](2.3.6-identity-notes.md#236127-before-060-uninstall-reinstall).
- **Status:** In force.

## 2.1.12.2 DNS-over-TLS on 853, answered by AdGuard

- **Decision:** AdGuard answers DoT with the DNS name's certificate (or the public wildcard), so encrypted queries are filtered too; 853 joins the ports only with the filter on; doctor checks DoH's `/dns-query`.
- **Shapes:** 1.12, 1.4
- **Made:** 2026-10-08, by the owner, for 0.6.3.
- **Why:** 2.3.1.11.6.
- **Status:** In force; being built for 0.6.3.
