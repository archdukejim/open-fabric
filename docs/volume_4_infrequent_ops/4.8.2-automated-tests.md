## 4.8.2.1 Running them

The suites live in `tests/<domain>/`, one per area, and run against real containers (the global Rule 9; the
layout is deviation D59): `sudo tests/run-all.sh [suite …]`, every suite when none is named. `tests/README.md`
lists what each suite proves. Moved from `AGENTS.md` §6 on 2026-10-03.

## 4.8.2.2 What a change needs

- Behaviour is proven against **real containers**, not mocks, wherever a container is involved (D57 lists the only
  stand-ins).
- Every feature adds to or extends a suite, including the refusal and security cases (what must be refused).
- A change is not done until the relevant suites, and `ruff` and `shellcheck` (D34), pass; results are reported
  faithfully, including what was not run (global Rule 9).

## 4.8.2.3 Lint

The `lint` suite (`tests/lint/run.sh`, also the `lint` workflow in GitHub Actions on every push and pull request)
runs ruff and shellcheck from their published images, pinned by digest, so nothing is installed on the host:

- **ruff** on `src/`, `scripts/` and `tests/` with the rules in the root `pyproject.toml`: pycodestyle and pyflakes,
  120 characters per line — except in `tests/`, whose one-line check messages may be longer.
- **shellcheck** at warning level on every shell script: the product's (`src/`, `packaging/`, `scripts/`) with no
  exception; the tests' with three exceptions their `eval`-run checks cause (SC2034, SC2097, SC2098).

Templates (`*.sh.j2` and the like) are not linted: they are not code until rendered (global Rule 1).
